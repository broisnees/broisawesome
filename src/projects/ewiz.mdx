eWiz is a native menu bar app for Apple silicon MacBooks that cares for the battery. Sitting full and running hot are what wear a battery out, so eWiz holds the charge where I set it, pauses charging when the battery gets warm, and controls what a closed Mac does. It was called Battlify until version 0.18.0; settings, licenses, history and the charge limit carried over.

## Features

- **A charge limit that holds.** Pick a ceiling between 50 and 100% and eWiz holds it, even while the Mac sleeps, by stopping charging just before sleep or by keeping the Mac awake on wall power.
- **Heat-aware charging.** Above the temperature I set, charging pauses, and the menu says why.
- **Sealed Sleep.** A closed Mac still wakes itself for the network and maintenance. Sealed Sleep switches those wakes off, turns Bluetooth and Wi-Fi off at lid close so earbuds stay with the phone, and shows what the last close cost. The tradeoff: opening the lid then takes 15 to 30 seconds.
- **Always Active.** Keeps the Mac running with the lid shut, on the charger or, if allowed, on battery, which also gives clamshell mode on an external display without the charger.
- **Automation rules.** Plug into a desk display and the limit drops to 80%; join the office Wi-Fi and the Mac stays awake.
- **AI agents, kept in check.** Claude, Cursor and other MCP apps can keep the Mac awake through a long build on a timer that ends by itself, and eWiz shows which agent holds it.
- **Schedules and Ready By.** Hold at the limit overnight and top up to full just before leaving, or charge on a weekly timetable.
- **History and details**: every charge and lid-closed session with drain per hour (exportable as CSV), plus health, cycle count, temperature and what the charger actually negotiated.
- **One-tap save modes**, a MagSafe light that shows charging or holding where the Mac allows it, and twelve menu bar battery icons.

## Requirements

Apple silicon Macs (M1 and later) on macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, including macOS 26 Tahoe. Intel Macs aren't supported. Charge limiting, Low Power Mode and the sleep controls need a small helper that runs as root, because macOS only lets root change them. It installs once from the menu and uninstalls from Settings › General.

## Install

Download it from ewiz.app, or install with Homebrew:

```bash
brew install --cask stroke-app/ewiz/ewiz
```

eWiz isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click the app in Applications and choose Open.
